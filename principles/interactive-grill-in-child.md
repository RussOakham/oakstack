# Principle: Interactive grill in child

**When it applies:** Product discovery, wayfinder, questionnaires, or any interview that needs human answers.

**Rule:** Grill / wayfinder runs in an **interactive child**. The human answers there. The hub only spawns and receives Plan-approved / Blocker reports.

**Stop when:** The parent thread starts collecting answers “for the child,” or a worker tries to finish the grill without the human.

**Cite in kickoffs as:** `interactive-grill-in-child`
